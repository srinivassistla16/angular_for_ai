A precise POSITIVE/ NEGATIVE sentiment analysis using Pydantic Parser.

final_chain = chain | branch_chain - Two chains are piped in this use case

First Chain generates the sentiment POSITIVE or NEGATIVE

branch_chain is a conditional chain.

If sentiment == POSITIVE - it invoves one sub-chain say positive_sub_chain

If sentiment == NEGATIVE - it invoves one sub-chain say positive_sub_chain

The outcome of the sub-chain shall be displayed in the bottom text area
